# Nexus360 Enterprise Feature Showcase

Nexus360 delivers an enterprise-ready, multi-vertical business automation engine:

---

## 1. Unified Omnichannel Lead Engine
* **Universal Contact Ingestion**: Seamless ingestion across digital campaigns, WhatsApp bots, social channels, web forms, and CSV imports.
* **AI Predictive Lead Scoring (0-100)**: Instant intent scoring with detailed explainability factors.
* **Pre-Flight Duplicate Detection & Automated Merge**: Guarantees zero duplicate leads in high-volume environments.

---

## 2. Multi-Industry CRM Verticals
* **Loan & DSA Banking CRM**: Complete banking workflow from loan application, EMI calculation, KYC verification, partner bank assignment, to final loan disbursal.
* **Real Estate & Plot Development CRM**: Township management, plot inventory allocation, interactive weekend site visit scheduling, booking agreements, and milestone payments.
* **Education & Admissions CRM**: Academic intake tracking, student qualification profiling, counselor conversion metrics, and fee collection.
* **Recruitment & Staffing CRM**: Candidate skills matching, job opening management, multi-round interview tracking, and staffing commission calculations.

---

## 3. Production AI & Autonomous Workflows
* **Conversational AI Assistant**: Real-time natural language query answering for pipeline value, team performance, and inventory.
* **Multi-Channel Copywriting Generator**: AI generates contextual WhatsApp, professional Email, and concise SMS follow-up templates in seconds.
* **Autonomous Trigger-Condition-Action Engine**: Executes multi-step workflows with millisecond audit logging.

---

## 4. Operational Excellence & Customer 360
* **Customer 360 Hub**: Centralizes lifetime value, multi-deal relations, and document vaults in a single interface.
* **Unified Document Vault**: Tenant-isolated file repository with KYC categorization.
* **Task & Calendar Hub**: Combines daily follow-ups, loan milestones, and site visits in an interactive calendar grid.
* **Multi-Domain Analytics**: Deep metrics across all 4 verticals with visual conversion funnels.
* **Admin Audit Trail**: Immutable compliance logs with actor attribution and JSON export.
